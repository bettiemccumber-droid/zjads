Zhijun Ads Manager

Zhijun Ads Manager is an internal advertising automation tool operated by Wenzhou Lucheng District Zhijun E-commerce Firm (温州市鹿城区志君电子商务商行). It is used only to manage our own Google Ads accounts, for advertising data analysis and campaign management. We do not sell Google Ads data. The tool does not generate AI content, including non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII).
