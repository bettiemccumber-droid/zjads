Zhijun Ads Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Google Ads API scope

Zhijun Ads Manager requests the Google Ads scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords. Through that scope the application accesses only these types of data from Google Ads accounts the firm owns or is authorized to manage:

  • Account and manager-account (MCC) identifiers, names, and relationships
  • Campaign structure, including campaigns, ad groups, and ads, with their identifiers, names, and status
  • Keywords and match types
  • Budgets, bids, and spend
  • Clicks, impressions, and conversion data
  • Ad assets used on those campaigns, such as sitelinks and callouts

The data is used only inside Zhijun Ads Manager, and only to provide advertising management, data viewing, and adjustment of the firm’s own campaigns. Zhijun Ads Manager complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. adwords data is not used for any purpose unrelated to that advertising management, including resale, industry-data analysis, or training models.

The data is not sold. It is not used to target advertisements to consumers. It is not used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. It is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, or other companies for their own use.

The data is stored in the application database hosted for the firm. Transmission is encrypted with HTTPS. Records are kept while the Google Ads account stays connected, and for no more than 24 months after that account is disconnected.

An authorized user can ask for deletion by emailing marshallmarsxybasi01318@gmail.com. We delete the requested Google Ads data, including stored API tokens, within 30 days of a verified request, unless a longer period is required by law.

Who we are

Zhijun Ads Manager is an internal Google Ads management tool operated by 温州市鹿城区志君电子商务商行 (Wenzhou Lucheng District Zhijun E-commerce Firm). This application and this privacy policy are published at https://www.zjads.top/. It is used only by personnel authorized by the firm to manage the firm’s own Google Ads accounts. It is not offered to the public and it is not a service for outside clients. No sign-in is required to read this policy.

Google Ads data we collect

When an authorized user connects a Google Ads account that the firm owns or is authorized to manage, Zhijun Ads Manager reads the following Google Ads data through the Google Ads API:

  • Google Ads customer IDs, account names, and manager-account (MCC) relationships
  • Campaign, ad group, and ad identifiers, names, and status
  • Campaign budgets and bidding settings, including CPC bids where the account exposes them
  • Cost and spend
  • Clicks, impressions, and conversions
  • Keywords and match types used in the firm’s own campaigns
  • Ad assets needed to operate those campaigns, such as sitelinks and callouts

Authorized users can also ask the tool to change the firm’s own campaigns through the Google Ads API. Those changes are limited to creating or updating campaigns, budgets, ad groups, responsive search ads, keywords, and ad assets such as sitelinks and callouts, and to adjusting budget or CPC bid settings. The tool does not change accounts the firm is not authorized to manage.

We request only the Google Ads data required to analyze and manage those accounts. We do not use the platform to access Google Ads accounts the firm is not authorized to manage. We do not collect Google user data for advertising to consumers, and we do not collect data from people who merely visit this public website.

How a Google account is connected

An employee authorized by the firm connects a Google Ads account on Google’s own consent screen and grants the Google Ads scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). We store the resulting refresh token only so the tool can call the Google Ads API for that account. The token is not used to open Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Photos, contacts, location, or YouTube. The employee can revoke the grant at any time from their Google Account. When the account is disconnected or the grant is revoked, we stop calling the API for it and delete the stored token within 30 days.

How we use the data

Google Ads data is used only to provide the user-facing features of Zhijun Ads Manager: the firm’s internal advertising optimization and performance analysis. That means reviewing budgets and spend, comparing clicks, impressions, and conversions, and managing the firm’s own campaigns. We do not use this data to build profiles for outside advertising, to resell audiences, or for any purpose other than the firm’s internal analysis and campaign management.

No sale or sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share Google Ads data or any other data collected by Zhijun Ads Manager. We do not transfer that data to advertisers, data brokers, or other companies for their own use, including for retargeting or interest-based advertising. Access stays inside the firm and is limited to authorized personnel for the internal purposes described in this policy.

The application and its database are hosted by an infrastructure provider. That provider stores and transmits the data only to operate the tool on our instructions. It may not use Google Ads data for its own products, for advertising, or to train models. Hosting the application is not a sale of data.

Limited Use of Google API data

Zhijun Ads Manager’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google Ads data only to provide and improve the internal campaign-management and performance-analysis features that are visible in this application.
  • We do not transfer Google Ads data to others, except to the infrastructure provider that hosts the tool and may use the data only on our instructions, where necessary to secure the service, where required by applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to the authorized users.
  • Humans at the firm read this data only when an authorized user asks for support, when it is necessary for security, or when it is required by law. We do not allow outside parties to read it.
  • We do not use Google Ads data to serve advertisements, to determine creditworthiness, or to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Data we do not collect

This application does not request Gmail, Google Drive, Google Photos, contacts, location, or YouTube data. Visitors to this public website are not asked to sign in with Google, and we do not collect their Google account data.

How data is stored

Google Ads data and the internal login records needed to operate the tool are stored on systems controlled by the firm. Traffic to the application is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Only authorized personnel can access stored data. We do not place this data in a public repository, and we do not make it available for download by outside parties.

Retention and deletion

While a Google Ads account stays connected, we keep its performance and campaign records so the firm can review budgets and results. When the account is disconnected, we stop collecting new data immediately. We do not keep Google Ads data for more than 24 months after disconnection. Authorized personnel can ask us to delete it sooner. We delete the requested Google Ads data, including stored API tokens, within 30 days of a verified request sent to marshallmarsxybasi01318@gmail.com, unless a longer period is required by law. Internal login records are deleted when the person’s access to the tool ends, or sooner on request, subject to the same legal exception.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Zhijun Ads Manager uses Google Ads data, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect. The current version is always published at https://www.zjads.top/privacy and linked from the homepage at https://www.zjads.top/.

No AI-generated content

Zhijun Ads Manager does not generate or distribute AI content. It does not create images, video, text for publication, or non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII). The application will not use Google APIs to generate non-consensual intimate imagery. The tool is limited to advertising data analysis and management of the firm’s own Google Ads campaigns.
